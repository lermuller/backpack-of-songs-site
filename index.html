How it works

Three things, and the app does not ask you for a fourth

Step 01

See what others left

Not a playlist — a memory. The song that marked a moment of their life, pinned where it happened. Each one sits on the ring closest to its true distance, pointing in its true direction.

Step 02

Leave your own

Whoever passes by next will find it. Put a line with it if you want, or say nothing and stay anonymous. It lands on other people's maps about an hour later.

Step 03

Keep the ones that stay with you

They go in your backpack, with the place and the person you got them from. That is the collection you go home with, and it counts the countries you passed through.

The real world

Discover the real world, and the songs that make it special.

Not another feed. No algorithm, no follower counts, nothing to scroll to the end of. You see what people left within 1.6 km of where you are standing — songs that meant something to someone, in the place they meant it.

Walk one street further and it is a different map. Go outside. The world is the interface.

An hour's delay

Nothing you post is visible to anyone for an hour, so nobody can follow you around in real time.

Never the exact spot

A post is rounded to about 100 m before the row is written. It does not record which building you were in.

Anonymity that holds

Post anonymously and the server never sends your name, your portrait or your country with it.

A map, not a feed

No algorithm, no follower counts, nothing to scroll to the end of. Just what is within 1.6 km.

All four are enforced in the database rather than by the app — a trigger and a policy, not a setting anyone can turn off.

Your music

No account, no login, nothing to connect

Searching for a song asks Apple's public music catalogue — that is where the title, the artist and the cover art come from. Tapping a cover opens the track in Spotify. That hand-off is a plain public link, not an integration, and it is the whole of the app's relationship with any music service.

What it does

  • Searches Apple's public catalogue for the title, the artist and the cover.
  • Keeps the link, so the song opens wherever the next person listens.
  • Opens the track in Spotify when you tap a cover.

What it never does

  • Play audio. It hands off, every time.
  • Ask you to connect a music account. There is nothing to connect.
  • Read what you are listening to, or post anywhere on your behalf.
