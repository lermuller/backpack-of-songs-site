Discover someone's song just by walking by

Step 01

See what others left

Not a playlist — a memory. The song that marked a moment of their life, pinned where it happened. Each one sits on the ring closest to its true distance, pointing in its true direction.

Step 02

Leave your own

Whoever passes by next will find it. Put a line with it if you want, or say nothing and stay anonymous. It lands on other people's maps about an hour later.

Step 03

Keep the ones
that stay with you

They go in your backpack, with the place and the person you got them from. That is the collection you go home with, and it counts the countries you passed through.
