2. What the app does

You leave the song you are listening to at the place you are standing. Other people passing near that place find it later, and can keep it in their backpack.

Three things follow from how it is built, and you should read them as part of the deal rather than as features:

  1. Nothing you post appears for an hour. Every post is held for one hour before anyone else can see it. This is enforced by the database, not by the app.
  2. Posts are visible to strangers near the place you left them. Anyone using the app within roughly 1.6 km of that point can see the track, the note you wrote, and — unless you posted anonymously — your display name, your chosen portrait and your country.
  3. The app does not play music. It shows track information and opens the track in Spotify. Playback, audio and your listening history are Spotify's, under Spotify's own terms.

The one-hour delay means nobody can follow you in real time, but it does not make a location private. Do not post from your home, your child's school, or anywhere you would not want a stranger to associate with you.
